## TraderSync Feature 6: MFE & MAE Analysis

### Simple explanation (MFE = Maximum Favorable Excursion, MAE = Maximum Adverse Excursion):

These fancy terms just mean:
- **MFE:** "How far DID the price go in your favor before you exited?" (Maximum profit you COULD have made)
- **MAE:** "How far did price go AGAINST you before coming back?" (Worst moment of the trade)

### Real-life example:
```
You buy NIFTY at 23,000
Price goes up to 23,200 (this is MFE — it went ₹200 in your favor)
Price then comes back to 23,050
You sell at 23,050 (your actual profit: ₹50)

MFE was ₹200 but you only captured ₹50.
You left ₹150 on the table!

If this pattern repeats: Your EXIT strategy is broken.
```

TraderSync shows MFE/MAE charts for ALL your trades → You can see if you're consistently exiting too early or too late.

# 🧠 Platform 2: Edgewonk
### The "Trading Psychology Master" Platform
**Website:** edgewonk.com | **Price:** ~€169 one-time (no monthly fee)

Edgewonk is built on one idea: **Most traders fail not because of bad strategy, but because of bad psychology.** Fear, greed, FOMO, revenge trading — these kill accounts, not bad charts.

---

## Edgewonk Feature 1: The Tiltmeter

### Simple explanation:

"Tilt" is a poker term. It means when you go emotionally off-track and start making stupid decisions. Happens to every trader after 2-3 consecutive losses.

The **Tiltmeter** is like a speedometer — but for your emotional state.

### How it works:
Edgewonk tracks patterns like:
- Are you trading more trades than normal? (Overtrading)
- Are your position sizes bigger after losses? (Revenge trading)
- Is your win rate suddenly dropping? (Emotional trading)

When these danger signs appear → The Tiltmeter turns RED.

### Visual (simple):
```
😊 Calm & Disciplined → Tiltmeter: Green (0-30%)
😤 Slightly Emotional → Tiltmeter: Yellow (30-60%)
😡 Full Tilt / DANGER → Tiltmeter: Red (60-100%)
```

When it goes red = STOP TRADING. Take a walk. Come back tomorrow.

### Why this is revolutionary:
No other tool in the world (including in India) has this. It's the first tool that tells you "Hey, your emotions are affecting your trading RIGHT NOW — stop before you blow up."

## Edgewonk Feature 2: Custom Mental Tags

### Simple explanation:
You know how when you're sick, you feel it but can't always explain it? Same with trading emotions.

Edgewonk lets you create your OWN emotion labels (tags). After every trade you answer:
- "What were you feeling when you took this trade?"

You pick from tags you've created:
- 😰 Fear of missing out (FOMO)
- 😤 Revenge trading after a loss
- 😐 Neutral / following plan
- 🤩 Overconfident
- 😨 Scared, hesitant
- 💪 Confident, clear-headed

After 3 months of tagging → Edgewonk shows you the correlation:
```
Emotion "FOMO" → Win rate: 28% (terrible!)
Emotion "Following Plan" → Win rate: 67% (great!)
```

Now you have DATA showing your emotions cost you money. Not just feelings. Actual numbers.

## Edgewonk Feature 3: Efficiency Score

### Simple explanation:
How well did you EXECUTE your strategy? Even if the strategy is good, if you execute poorly, you lose money.

Think of it like cricket:
- You have a plan: "Hit through the covers"
- Did you execute it? Or did you panic and slog?

Your **Efficiency Score** measures: Out of all the times you were supposed to follow your rules, what % did you actually follow them?

```
90%+ Efficiency = Excellent discipline (Green)
70-90% = Good, some slippage (Yellow)
Below 70% = Your emotions are overriding your plan (Red)
```

## Chartlog Feature 4: Exit Quality Analysis

### Simple explanation:

```

Exit Efficiency = Your profit / Maximum possible profit
```
